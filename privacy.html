Privacy Policy

Channel Autopilot · Last updated 23 November 2026

Summary

Channel Autopilot is a private tool used by its operator to upload videos to the operator's own YouTube channels. It has no public sign-up, collects no data about viewers or the public, and shares no data with anyone.

YouTube API Services

Channel Autopilot uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a channel, the channel owner agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Information accessed

  • Authorization tokens. When a channel owner signs in with Google, the tool receives an OAuth token limited to the youtube.upload permission.
  • Upload results. The ID and scheduled time of each video the tool uploads.
  • Public video information. Public metadata (title, channel name, licence, duration) of videos considered as sources, read with the YouTube Data API.

The tool does not read a connected channel's private data, analytics, comments, subscribers or viewing history.

How the information is used

Solely to upload and schedule videos on the connected channels, to credit source creators, and to avoid uploading the same clip twice. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

To choose which passage of a source video to excerpt, its transcript and public title are processed by an AI text service (Anthropic). No authorization tokens and no data from the connected channels are sent to it.

Storage and sharing

Tokens and upload records are stored on a server controlled by the operator. They are not sold, shared or transferred to third parties. Tokens are kept only while a channel remains connected. Public information about source videos that were considered but not used is deleted after 30 days.

Revoking access and deleting data

A channel owner can revoke access at any time at Google's security settings page. Once access is revoked the stored token stops working and is deleted from the server. To request deletion of any stored records, use the contact below.
