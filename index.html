Channel Autopilot

A private publishing tool that prepares short videos and uploads them, on a schedule, to its operator's own YouTube channels.

What it does

  • Finds long-form videos that may be reused: Creative Commons (CC BY) videos and videos from creators who have given permission.
  • Cuts short clips from them and credits the original creator in every description.
  • Uploads the clips to the operator's YouTube channels at scheduled times.

Who can use it

Channel Autopilot is not a public service. It is operated by one owner and connects only to YouTube channels that the owner controls and has signed in to.

How it uses YouTube

Channel Autopilot uses YouTube API Services to read the public licence and details of source videos and to upload the operator's finished videos. It requests a single permission, youtube.upload, and uses it only to upload and schedule videos on the connected channels. See the privacy policy and terms of service for details.
