What this is

Channel Autopilot is a private command-line tool. Its operator uses it to prepare short videos and to upload them to YouTube channels the operator owns. It is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and is not sold or licensed to anyone.

YouTube Terms of Service

Channel Autopilot uses YouTube API Services. Anyone who connects a channel to it agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The tool is operated in line with the YouTube API Services Terms of Service and Developer Policies.

Who may connect a channel

Only the operator, and only for channels the operator owns and has signed in to with Google. Access can be withdrawn at any time at Google's security settings page.

What gets uploaded

  • Only videos the operator has the right to publish: edited excerpts of videos their owners have marked Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY) on YouTube, or that their owners have given written permission to reuse.
  • Every upload names the original speaker, title and channel, links to the full source, states the licence and lists the changes made.
  • No upload is presented as endorsed by the original speaker or channel.

Rights concerns and corrections

If you own a video that was excerpted and want it corrected or removed, open an issue or comment on the upload. It will be corrected or taken down.

The tool and this website are provided as they are, without warranty of any kind. Videos on the connected channels are for education and are not financial or professional advice.
